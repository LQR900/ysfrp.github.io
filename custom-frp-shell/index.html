Manufacturing Process

01

Design Review

Analyze your drawings, 3D models, frequency requirements and operating environment with our engineering team.

02

Mold Making

CNC-milled or hand-built precision mold created from the approved design for repeatable production.

03

Layup & Curing

Vacuum infusion or hand layup with controlled resin-to-fiber ratio and controlled curing cycle.

04

Trimming & Finishing

CNC trimming, surface finishing, gel coat or paint application and mounting point preparation.

05

QC & Delivery

Dimensional inspection, functional test, secure export packaging and worldwide shipping.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you manufacture FRP shells from customer drawings?

Yes. Send us CAD files, PDF drawings or a physical sample. Our engineers evaluate the design, propose mold and layup, and produce an accurate FRP shell from prototype to volume.

What is the minimum order for custom FRP shells?

We accept prototype orders from 1 piece. Batch and mass-production pricing scales favorably with quantity, and we support ongoing recurring supply.

What surface finishes are available for FRP shells?

We offer gel coat (matte or gloss), 2K painted finishes in any RAL color, textured surfaces and anti-static or fire-retardant options depending on application.

Do you offer CNC mold making for custom FRP shells?

Yes. We operate in-house CNC machining to produce precision molds from your 3D models, ensuring repeatable, dimensionally stable shells across production runs.

How long does custom FRP shell production take?

Prototypes are typically delivered in 7-15 days after design confirmation. Standard production runs take 20-30 days depending on complexity and order size.
