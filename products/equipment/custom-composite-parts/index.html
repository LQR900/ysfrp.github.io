Frequently Asked Questions

Can you make custom composite parts from our drawings?

Yes, we specialize in custom composite part manufacturing from technical drawings. Send us your CAD files (STEP, IGES, STL, SolidWorks, CATIA), PDF drawings, or even hand sketches, and our engineering team will review the design, optimize it for composite manufacturing, and provide a competitive quotation within 48 hours.

What composite materials do you work with?

We work with a wide range of composite materials: E-glass FRP, S-2 glass FRP, E-carbon / CFRP (3K, 6K, 12K carbon fiber), aramid fiber (Kevlar), hybrid fiber composites, and sandwich panel constructions with foam or honeycomb cores. We select the optimal material system based on your mechanical, thermal, and environmental requirements.

What is the minimum order quantity?

For custom composite parts, we accept prototype orders starting from just 1 piece. Our flexible MOQ policy is designed to support product development, R&D projects, and startups alongside large-volume production orders. Competitive pricing begins at 5+ pieces for most standard parts.

Do you offer OEM/ODM services?

Absolutely. We are an experienced OEM/ODM manufacturer providing complete custom composite part services including material selection consultation, structural design optimization, mold making, production, branding, and packaging. We work with companies across 50+ countries in diverse industries from aerospace to consumer products.

What surface finishes are available for composite parts?

We offer comprehensive surface finishing options: Class-A gel coat (smooth, paintable), 2K automotive paint (any RAL color), clear coat for carbon fiber (natural or tinted), textured non-slip finish, metallic finish, and high-gloss piano finish. For structural parts, we also offer machined-to-tolerance surfaces with no cosmetic finish.

What is the typical lead time for custom parts?

Prototype parts: 7-15 days depending on complexity and mold requirements. Production orders: 20-30 days. Rush prototyping available at additional cost. For large projects requiring new mold fabrication, add 5-10 days for mold making. We maintain transparent communication throughout the production process.
