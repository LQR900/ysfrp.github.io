Manufacturing Process

01

Design Review

Review charger dimensions, ventilation requirements, cable routing, display placement, and branding specifications. Create optimized FRP design.

02

Mold Making

Create precision molds with integrated ventilation louvers, cable glands, and mounting boss locations. Multi-part molds for complex geometries.

03

Layup & Curing

Vacuum infusion for consistent fiber-resin ratio and void-free structure. Weather-grade resin systems with UV inhibitors throughout the laminate.

04

Finishing & Sealing

Gel coat application, CNC trimming, ventilation grill cutting, gasket groove machining, and IP-seal testing before surface finishing.

05

QC & Delivery

IP65 leak testing, dimensional inspection, surface quality check, secure foam packaging, and worldwide shipping.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you make EV charger covers from custom designs?

Yes. We specialize in custom EV charger cover designs. Send us your technical drawings, CAD files, or specifications, and our engineering team will design and manufacture a custom FRP EV charger enclosure that fits your exact dimensions and design requirements.

What IP rating do your EV charger covers provide?

Our standard FRP EV charger covers are IP65 rated for outdoor use, providing complete protection against dust and low-pressure water jets from any direction. Higher IP66 and IP67 ratings are available with enhanced gasket systems for locations with extreme weather exposure.

Are your EV charger enclosures weather and UV resistant?

Absolutely. Our EV charger covers feature UV-resistant gel coat with UV inhibitors that prevent fading, cracking, and degradation even after years of direct sunlight exposure. The FRP material is also corrosion-proof and maintains its structural integrity in temperatures from -40掳C to +80掳C.

Do you offer OEM services for EV charging equipment brands?

Yes, we are an experienced OEM partner for EV charging station manufacturers worldwide. We provide full OEM services including custom branding, logo integration, brand-matched colors, and bespoke design. Our production capacity ranges from single prototype units to thousands of production enclosures annually.

What is the minimum order quantity?
