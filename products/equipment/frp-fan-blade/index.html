Material Advantages of FRP Fan Blades

Superior Corrosion Resistance

FRP is inherently immune to rust, corrosion from acids, alkalis, saltwater, and most industrial chemicals. Outlasts metal blades in harsh environments by 3-5x.

40-60% Lighter Than Metal

Significantly reduced blade mass means lower bearing loads, reduced motor power consumption, and longer equipment life.

High-Strength Performance

Custom fiber orientation engineering delivers superior tensile and flexural strength. Blades maintain structural integrity at high RPMs.

Low Inertia, High Efficiency

Lower rotating mass enables faster start-stop cycles, improving system responsiveness and energy efficiency in variable-speed applications.

Lower Total Cost of Ownership

Longer service life, reduced maintenance, no corrosion replacement costs, and lower energy bills make FRP blades more economical long-term.

Non-Magnetic & Non-Sparking

FRP is naturally non-magnetic and non-sparking 鈥?ideal for sensitive environments, MRI facilities, and explosive atmosphere (ATEX) zones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you manufacture custom fan blades from our drawings?

Yes. We can manufacture custom FRP fan blades from your technical drawings, CAD models, or even an existing sample. Send us your dimensional requirements, airflow specs, RPM, and operating environment, and our engineering team will design an optimized fan blade solution for you.

What materials are used for FRP fan blades?

We use high-quality E-glass fiberglass or S-2 glass fibers with premium resin systems. Options include: isophthalic polyester resin (standard), vinyl ester resin (corrosion-resistant), epoxy resin (high-performance), and fire-retardant resins for hazardous environments. Core materials can include foam or balsa wood for large blades.

What is the maximum size for FRP fan blades?

We can manufacture fan blades up to 5 meters in diameter. Standard production range covers 200mm to 5,000mm diameter blades. For very large fans, we use segmented blade construction for easier transport and assembly while maintaining structural integrity.

Do you offer OEM services for fan manufacturers?

Yes, we are an experienced OEM partner for industrial fan manufacturers, HVAC companies, and marine equipment producers. We offer branded fan blades with custom colors, logos, and packaging. Our production capacity supports both small prototype runs and large-scale OEM orders with consistent quality.

What is the typical lead time?

Prototype fan blades: 7-15 days. Production orders: 20-30 days depending on quantity and complexity. Rush prototyping is available at additional cost. We also maintain standard blade inventories for common sizes to support urgent replacement needs.
