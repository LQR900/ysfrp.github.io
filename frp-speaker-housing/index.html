FRP Speaker Housing (Fiberglass Speaker Enclosures)

High-performance fiberglass reinforced plastic (FRP) speaker housings engineered for professional audio applications. Our speaker enclosures offer superior weather resistance, acoustic performance optimization, and lightweight construction compared to traditional wood or metal enclosures. Suitable for indoor concert halls, outdoor PA systems, marine audio installations, and bass guitar cabinets.

Technical Specifications

MaterialFiberglass Reinforced Polymer (FRP / GFRP)
DimensionsCustom sizes from 100mm to 2,000mm (custom available)
Wall Thickness3mm – 12mm (depending on size and acoustic requirements)
Weight60% lighter than steel, 30% lighter than wood (comparable stiffness)
Finish OptionsUV gel coat, 2K paint, textured, high-gloss piano finish
Color OptionsWhite (standard), RAL custom colors available
Weather ResistanceIP65 rated (with sealing), UV-stable, corrosion-proof
Acoustic TuningAvailable with internal acoustic damping panels
MountingWall mount, pole mount, flyware, stacking options

Material Advantages of FRP Speaker Housing

Lightweight

60% lighter than steel enclosures — reduces shipping costs and simplifies installation, especially for ceiling and wall-mounted applications.

Weather & Corrosion Resistant

Immune to rust, rot, and moisture damage. UV-resistant gel coat prevents fading and cracking under outdoor exposure conditions.

Acoustic Performance

FRP shells offer superior internal acoustic damping compared to wood, reducing unwanted resonances for cleaner, more accurate sound reproduction.

Design Flexibility

Mold into virtually any shape — curved, angular, slim-profile, or complex geometries impossible with metal or wood fabrication.

Surface Finish Quality

Class-A surface finish straight from the mold. Achieve premium paint adhesion, textures, and multi-color designs in a single production step.

Mass Production Ready

Low per-unit cost at scale. Competitive pricing for high-volume OEM orders with consistent quality across all units.

Manufacturing Process

01

Design Review

Review your acoustic requirements, dimensional specs, and mounting needs. 3D CAD models generated and acoustic simulations performed.

02

Mold Making

Create precision FRP or metal molds for your custom speaker housing geometry. Prototyping molds ready in 3-5 days.

03

Layup & Curing

Vacuum infusion or hand layup using engineered resin systems and high-quality fiberglass mat. Controlled curing for optimal mechanical properties.

04

Trimming & Finishing

CNC trimming, hole cutting for drivers and mounting points, surface sanding, and gel coat or paint application.

05

QC & Delivery

Dimensional inspection, acoustic testing (optional), secure foam-lined packaging, worldwide shipping to 50+ countries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you make speaker housings from custom designs?

Yes, we specialize in custom speaker housing designs. Send us your technical drawings, CAD files, or samples, and our engineering team will design and manufacture a custom FRP speaker enclosure that meets your exact acoustic and structural requirements.

What surface finishes are available for speaker housings?

We offer multiple finish options: UV-resistant gel coat (white or RAL colors), 2K automotive paint, textured finish, and high-gloss piano finish. All finishes are designed to withstand outdoor exposure and maintain acoustic performance.

Are your speaker housings weather-resistant?

Absolutely. Our FRP speaker housings are designed for outdoor and marine environments. They feature UV-resistant gel coat, corrosion-proof construction, and IP65-rated sealing options, making them ideal for outdoor PA systems, marine speakers, and all-weather installations.

Do you offer OEM/ODM services for speaker manufacturers?

Yes, we provide full OEM/ODM services including custom branding, logo printing, custom color matching, and bespoke acoustic tuning. We work with audio equipment brands worldwide to produce branded speaker enclosures at competitive factory-direct pricing.

What is the minimum order quantity?

For prototype and custom designs, we accept orders starting from just 1 piece. For standard production runs, MOQ starts from 5 pieces. We offer highly competitive pricing for larger volume orders and provide flexible MOQ terms for startups and emerging brands.
